First and foremost, I want this blog to be something I can truly call my own, I want to be able to understand every piece of it slowly being able to understand how and why this collection of fucking crazy files actually functions. I want to be able to customize every post and every small piece to my liking and to have a creative outlet to be able to do amazing wonderful things. I want something on the internet that is *mine*.

Second, I want this to basically be a diary for myself, I want to have this outlet to share parts of me. But not making it about being honest with every fucking detail but just having a place to share and to love myself. I want to be able to post pictures and just be all around sick as fuck.

Finally, I really want to be consistent. I fall into a lot of traps of forgetting about projects and pushing them off when I am not crazy autistic about them, but I really want to just be fucking able to do this one little thing, because how fucking cool will it be to show my kids this sick ass website I have been updating since before they were born! like come fucking on bitch thats so exciting.

I'm sure I have other things I want out of this experience but for now I gotta go to bed.
