I feel like today I just really am tired I just feel so uninterested in life right now. Like I don't give a shit about school or my jobs or my health or anything and goddamn in I just want to sleep.

Which I know is just some depression talking but it still frustrating and hard not to just fully give in to and sleep all day and just do nothing. Because I want to be successful but I really don't want to do any actions to get there. But I do want to get those things.

Goddamn I feel like I kinda just want to die, not in a super suicidal way just in a disaffected way, like I don't care what the hell happens and I just want to see the world pass me by and forget about me.

...

But now I am crying because goddamn I *want* to be remembered. 

I *want* to feel loved.

I *want* to be a person.

...I forget that sometimes.


I love you, I love me.
